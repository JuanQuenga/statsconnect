Contact

Last updated October 6, 2026.

StatsConnect is run by one developer, and email is the fastest way to reach me: harmiox@gmail.com. Real messages from real users get read and answered — bug reports in particular are genuinely appreciated.

What to include

  • For a bug or wrong number: the page URL, what you saw, and what you expected. Screenshots help.
  • For a missing or mis-attributed battle: the player tag and roughly when it happened.
  • For feature ideas: the game, the job you’re trying to do, and what you’d do with it.

What to expect

Most mail gets a reply within a few days. Aggregation fixes land in the next daily rebuild; anything bigger gets an answer about whether and when it’s planned. There’s no support hotline or chat — email keeps everything in one place.

Data requests

To delete your account data (sign-in identity and connected player tags), email harmiox@gmail.com from the address on the account with “delete my data” in the subject, or disconnect profiles yourself from your account settings. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. A copy of your stored data is available on the same address.

Billing

Support contributions are handled by Stripe. For receipt or refund questions, use the receipt link in your email, the Stripe billing portal, or email me and I’ll take care of it.

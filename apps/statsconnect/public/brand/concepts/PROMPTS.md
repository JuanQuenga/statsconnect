# StatsConnect logo concept prompts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The existing StatsConnect mark and Supercell-themed hero artwork were supplied as visual references.

## Concept 1 — Interlocking S

Create an original, premium mobile-game logo system for `StatsConnect`: a bold shield-like emblem built from two interlocking rounded game tiles that form an S in negative space, with one small gold connection point. Use royal purple, electric blue, strong dark outlines, restrained bevels, and a clean wordmark. Include a standalone favicon preview. Keep the silhouette readable at 16 px. Avoid network diagrams, charts, excessive neon, chrome, crypto styling, mascots, crowns, and copied publisher typography.

## Concept 2 — SC monogram

Create an original, premium mobile-game logo system for `StatsConnect`: a compact rounded-hexagonal badge containing an abstract SC monogram made from a continuous thick ribbon, with a small coral arena-bridge connector at its center. Use cobalt blue, violet, off-white, and a strong ink-dark outline. Include the horizontal wordmark, app icon, and favicon test. Keep the mark recognizable in monochrome and readable at 16 px. Avoid glowing orbs, network imagery, charts, chrome, cyberpunk styling, mascots, crowns, and copied publisher typography.

## Concept 3 — Arena token

Create an original, premium mobile-game logo system for `StatsConnect`: a diamond-shaped collectible arena token split into cyan and magenta-violet fields, connected by a white bridge and small gold pin that subtly suggests an S path. Use chunky outlines, a flat-vector foundation, and subtle clay-like depth. Include the wordmark, isolated badge, and 32 px favicon. Avoid neon, chrome, nodes, charts, arrows, infinity symbols, esports crests, mascots, crowns, and clutter.

## Selected mark extraction

Refine Concept 2 into one clean standalone SC monogram. Preserve the interlocking cobalt-blue S, royal-violet C, tiny coral connector, and deep-navy rounded-hexagonal outline. Simplify shading, use crisp vector-first geometry, center the mark with even padding, and omit all text. Render on a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background without shadows, reflections, texture, or green in the subject so the background can be removed cleanly.
